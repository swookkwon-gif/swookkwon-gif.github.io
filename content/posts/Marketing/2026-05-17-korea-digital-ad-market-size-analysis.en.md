## 1. Overall Advertising and Digital Advertising Market Size in Korea for 2025

### 📊 Summary of Figures
* **Total Advertising Market Size:** Approx. **17.2 Trillion KRW (~$12.1B)**
* **Digital (Online/Mobile) Advertising Size:** Approx. **10.3 Trillion KRW (~$7.2B, roughly 60% of total)**

### 🧠 Basis of Calculation and Explanation
* **Data Sources:** Annual broadcasting and telecommunications advertising expenditure survey reports by KOBACO (Korea Broadcast Advertising Corp.), and Cheil Worldwide's total advertising expenditure settlement data.
* **Calculation Logic:** The total advertising expenditure in Korea for 2023 was tallied at around 16.7 trillion KRW (~$11.7B), and 2024 showed a mild growth of around 2-3% (approx. 17.1 trillion KRW, ~$12.0B). Reflecting a similar low-growth trend of around 2% for 2025, it is estimated to reach **approx. 17.2 trillion KRW (~$12.1B)**.
* Among this, the proportion of digital media (PC+Mobile) has steadily risen, firmly establishing its share at the 60% mark. $17.2 \times 0.6 = 10.32$, meaning **approx. 10.3 trillion KRW (~$7.2B)** is the share for the digital advertising market. Upon entering maturity, rather than explosive double-digit growth, the market is focusing on quality growth through AI-based optimization, DOOH (Digital Out-Of-Home), and the expansion of retail media.

### 4.2 Execution & Growth Metrics — Execution Goals and Growth Indicators

#### 📈 Campaign Projections: Quarterly Campaign Launch Targets

**Why is this metric important? (Industry Standard Metric)**
In the AdTech and DSP (Demand-Side Platform) business, the 'number of active campaigns' and 'number of new launches' transcend simple sales metrics and stand as the most powerful **Leading Indicators** proving a platform's growth potential and the health of its deep learning ecosystem. Because revenue is ultimately determined by `Number of Campaigns × Average Budget per Campaign`, global B2B AdTech companies like Criteo and Moloco rigorously manage this as their top priority core KPI.

**The Meaning of a 'Campaign Launch' and Expected Execution Scale**
*   **Meaning:** A campaign here is not a one-off banner ad. It signifies the **activation of an 'Always-on' performance marketing campaign** where client internal data (product catalogs, app trackers, etc.) is integrated, and the algorithm conducts 24/7 real-time bidding (RTB) to maximize conversions (Purchase/Install).
*   **Minimum and Average Scale and Basis of Calculation (Industry Standard):** 
    *   **Minimum Test Budget (10 Million KRW / month, ~$7,030):** The mathematical minimum baseline in the industry for deep learning models to pass the initial Cold-Start phase.
        *   **Calculation Basis:** For an AI algorithm to build predictive models, a minimum of '100 significant conversion (Purchase) data points per week' is essential. Conservatively estimating the average Cost Per Action (CPA) in the Korean e-commerce/app market at 25,000 KRW (~$17.6), it yields **`400 conversions/month × 25,000 KRW (CPA) = 10,000,000 KRW (~$7,030)`**. Below this amount, deep learning training is mathematically unviable, which is why Criteo, Google DV360, and others require this as a practical Minimum Commitment.
    *   **Average Execution Budget (50 Million KRW / month, ~$35,170):** The average Scale-up size an account settles into once ROAS performance is proven ("Prove It") through a 30-day A/B test.
        *   **Calculation Basis:** The average monthly digital performance advertising expenditure for mid-to-large e-commerce/O2O advertisers in Korea is approximately 300 million KRW (~$211K). It is an industry standard media mix to allocate 15%~20% of the total budget to 'Dynamic Retargeting (DSP)' media, which typically yields the highest conversion efficiency. Thus, **`300 Million KRW (Total Budget) × ~16.6% = 50 Million KRW (~$35,170)`** is calculated as the average execution amount per campaign.
    *   **Conclusion:** Therefore, launching 1 new campaign does not mean a one-off sale, but signifies integrating a stable 'Lock-in pipeline' worth **600 million KRW annually (50M KRW × 12 months, ~$422K)** into the platform.

Position ourselves as a specialized solution partner in the separate KPI domain of performance (ROAS) optimization, not the branding budgets they manage.
* **Measurement Partner Integration:** Remove entry barriers by strengthening SDK integrations with major Mobile Measurement Partners (MMP) like AppsFlyer, Adjust, and Branch, allowing gaming/app vertical advertisers to immediately onboard RTB House campaigns without altering their existing measurement infrastructure.

## 📊 Conclusion and Implications

1. **Winner-Takes-All Platforms:** The oligopoly where the 4 Big Techs (Naver, Google, Kakao, Meta) sweep up **over 80% (over 8 Trillion KRW / ~$5.6B+)** of the 10 trillion KRW (~$7.0B) Korean digital advertising market remains solid.
2. **The Value of Proprietary Technology:** Countless AdTech companies compete for the remaining 2 trillion KRW (~$1.4B) pie. The commonality among Criteo, RTB House, and Moloco—who survive and generate tens of billions of KRW (tens of millions of dollars) in revenue here—is that they possess irreplaceable technological prowess in the form of **powerful 'Deep Learning / Machine Learning Recommendation Algorithms'**, not merely acting as media proxies.

Ultimately, the future advertising market will see an even clearer polarization, where only those who own the ad inventory space and those who possess 'ultra-large AI models' that accurately predict which users to match to that space will survive.
