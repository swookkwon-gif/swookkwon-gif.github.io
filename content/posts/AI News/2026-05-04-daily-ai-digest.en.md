## 1. Google Announces Massive Expansion in AI Healthcare and New Partnerships

Google recently announced a large-scale expansion plan in the AI healthcare sector, unveiling new partnerships with several medical institutions and pharmaceutical companies. This expansion focuses on utilizing AI technology to improve disease diagnosis accuracy, accelerate the drug discovery process, and provide personalized treatment solutions. While this is expected to intensify competition in the medical AI market and stimulate investment from other Big Tech companies, data privacy and ethical considerations will become increasingly important.

## 2. Microsoft Materializes In-House AI Chip Production Plans and Strengthens Foundry Collaborations

Microsoft announced that it is materializing its plans to produce in-house AI chips and strengthening cooperation with major foundry companies. This is part of a strategy to maximize the AI workload processing efficiency of its cloud service, Azure, and reduce reliance on external suppliers like NVIDIA. By designing its own chips, Microsoft aims to secure AI computing power specialized for the Azure cloud environment, targeting cost reduction and performance enhancement, which could lead to shifts in the competitive landscape of the AI chip market.

## 3. NVIDIA Unveils Next-Gen GPU Architecture 'Blackwell Ultra' and Presents AI Supercomputing Vision

NVIDIA officially announced its next-generation GPU architecture, 'Blackwell Ultra', presenting a new vision for AI supercomputing. Blackwell Ultra further elevates the performance of the existing Blackwell architecture, focusing on dramatically improving the computational power required for training and inferencing large language models (LLMs). This holds the potential to accelerate the pace of AI research and development, enable the emergence of new AI applications, and further solidify NVIDIA's dominance in the AI infrastructure market.

## 5. Meta Acquires Humanoid Robot Startup ARI: Strengthening Embodied AI

Meta has acquired Assured Robot Intelligence (ARI), an AI humanoid robot startup. ARI's founders will join Meta's Superintelligence Labs to integrate their robot control and autonomous learning technologies, aiming to strengthen "physical world AI models." This acquisition clearly demonstrates an expansion beyond LLMs into the field of 'Embodied AI' and is evaluated as a strategic move by Meta to explore the applicability of AI in the real world, beyond virtual reality (VR) and augmented reality (AR).

## 7. The Rise of Open Source LLMs: DeepSeek V4 Pro Performance Proven and Xiaomi MiMo-V2.5-Pro Released

The DeepClaude project demonstrated the technical maturity of open-source LLMs by proving that DeepSeek V4 Pro can replace core functionalities of Anthropic's Claude Code model at a 17x lower cost. According to benchmarks by the US Center for AI Standards and Innovation (CAISI), DeepSeek V4 Pro is about 8 months behind leading US models but showed nearly equal performance in mathematics. Furthermore, Xiaomi released MiMo-V2.5-Pro, a powerful new open-source model, contributing to the expansion of the open-source LLM ecosystem.

## 8. Evolution of AI Agent Technology: Open Source Platforms Launched and the Importance of 'Skill' Encoding

AI agent technology is gaining attention as the next stage of LLMs. Open-source agent engineering platforms like PandaProbe have been launched, helping developers easily build and deploy agents. Additionally, to prevent AI coding agents from missing nuanced aspects like a senior engineer's intuition, it is becoming crucial to make them autonomous and reliable in professional environments through 'agent skills' or 'discipline encoding'. This expands the frontier of AI engineering into intelligent and context-aware problem-solving.

## 9. Social Impact of AI: Chinese Court Halts AI Firings and Controversy Over Healthcare Premiums in Kenya

A court in Hangzhou, China, set a precedent against mass layoffs caused by AI by ruling it illegal and ordering compensation for a company that fired quality assurance engineers, claiming an LLM had replaced their jobs. Meanwhile, in Kenya, an issue arose where an AI-based predictive machine learning algorithm overestimated the income of the poor during the process of setting health insurance premiums, leading to higher charges. This exposed the risks of AI algorithm bias and the exacerbation of social inequality.

## 10. Open Source AI Infrastructure Innovation: Tenstorrent-based LLM Inference and Alibaba FlashQLA Released

Moreh, a company specializing in AI infrastructure, proved that the inference performance of the latest open-source LLMs, such as DeepSeek, is equal to or greater than NVIDIA's 'DGX A100' class by applying its in-house developed 'MoAI inference framework' to Tenstorrent's 'Galaxy Wormhole' system. Along with this, Alibaba open-sourced 'FlashQLA', a high-performance linear attention kernel library that accelerates LLM inference speed by up to 3 times on NVIDIA 'Hopper' GPUs, contributing to increasing the efficiency and diversity of AI infrastructure.
