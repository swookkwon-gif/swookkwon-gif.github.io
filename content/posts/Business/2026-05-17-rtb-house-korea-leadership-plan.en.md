> **"Don't manage — coach. Don't dictate — bridge. Don't assume — listen."**

A Country Manager at a global AdTech company in Korea must simultaneously navigate two worlds: **Global Integration with the Warsaw headquarters' standards** and **Local Responsiveness optimized for the Korean market** — striking the right balance between these two determines success or failure.

This plan is a practical roadmap for the incoming Country Manager leading RTB House Korea's 20-person organization (Sales, AM, TAM, TA, Inventory, Admin) to **earn the team's trust within the first 6 months** and **drive revenue growth through a concise, clearly executable framework**.

---

## 4.1 First 6 Months & Onboarding — 30-60-90-180 Day Plan

We extend the standard **30-60-90 Day Framework** used in global B2B sales leadership to a **30-60-90-180 day** four-phase design, adapted for the relationship-driven business characteristics of the Korean market.

### 🚩 Phase 0: Challenge Identification

The three biggest risks the incoming Country Manager will face in the Korean market:

| # | Risk | Risk Context | Impact if Realized |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | **Loss of Core Assets & Key Talent** | The most critical risk during a leadership transition is failing to fully defend and leverage the Korean branch's established core assets and top talent (key personnel attrition) | Loss of organizational momentum & pipeline collapse |
| 2 | **Insufficient Transfer of Global Winning Experiences** | More than a simple HQ-branch perspective gap, the bigger problem is that proven 'winning experiences and learnings' from other global markets are not sufficiently transferred and localized to the Korean market | Growth ceiling & repeated inefficiencies |
| 3 | **Right Person, Right Build Failure** | During aggressive business and team expansion, there is a risk of failing to recruit the right talent (Right Person) aligned with organizational culture and market challenges, and failing to build them up properly | Resource waste & delayed market penetration |

---

### 📅 Phase 1: Listen & Learn (Day 1–30) — "Understand the Machine"

> **Principle:** Change nothing. Observe and listen.

**3 Action Items (Only 3):**

1.  **1:1 Listening Tour (All 20 members, completed within 3 weeks)**
    *   Ask exactly 3 key questions:
        * "What is the biggest bottleneck in your work right now?"
        * "What support from leadership is most urgently needed?"
        * "What is your proudest recent win?"
    *   ✅ **Korea Optimization:** In Korean organizations, 1:1 meetings are easily misinterpreted as 'evaluations.' Always declare that **"this is listening, not evaluation"** and use informal settings like coffee chats or lunch meetings.

2.  **Shadow Week (1 week of field observation)**
    *   Directly observe at least 2 client meetings with Sales and at least 2 campaign optimization sessions with AM. Do not intervene — only take notes.
    *   ✅ **Purpose:** Get a firsthand 'feel' for CRM data health, actual usage of sales methodologies (MEDDIC, etc.), and how clients perceive RTB House.

3.  **Secure 1 Quick Win**
    *   Within 30 days, resolve one important issue that the team has been struggling with but couldn't get resolved. (e.g., clearing delayed leadership approvals, streamlining work processes, resolving a client issue)
    *   ✅ **Effect:** Creates the impression that "this person shows through actions, not words."

---

### 📅 Phase 2: Assess & Align (Day 31–60) — "Establish the Structure"

> **Principle:** Transition from observation to diagnosis. Present direction to both HQ and the team.

**3 Action Items (Direct Response to Phase 0 Core Risks):**

1.  **Key Talent Retention & Leverage Plan (→ Risk #1 Response)**
    *   Based on insights from the Phase 1 Listening Tour, diagnose each team member's **Competency Map** and **Flight Risk Level**:
        * **① Identify Core Assets:** Document at the individual level the unique capabilities (client relationships, technical expertise, market insights) the organization possesses.
        * **② Retention Action Plan:** For senior-level members with high flight risk, immediately conduct 1:1 career conversations presenting specific growth paths (role expansion, global project participation, compensation adjustments, etc.).
        * **③ Structuring Knowledge Leverage:** Begin converting tacit knowledge accumulated by individuals — key client histories, deal-closing know-how, market insights — into team-accessible formats (playbooks, CRM records, shared wikis).
    *   ✅ **Why first?** If talent leaves during a leadership transition, recovery takes 6 months to a year. Instilling the conviction that "I can grow in this organization" by Day 60 is the prerequisite for all strategies.

2.  **Global Winning Playbook Import System (→ Risk #2 Response)**
    *   Design a pipeline to systematically transfer proven success stories and learnings from HQ and other markets (DACH, UK, France, Japan, etc.) to Korea:
        * **① Global Best Practice Audit:** Through 1:1 calls with Regional Directors at HQ and key markets, collect the Top 3 most effective plays from each market (e.g., specific vertical attack strategies, scale-up tactics, churn defense strategies).
        * **② Korea Localization Filter:** Classify collected global plays into those that can and cannot be localized for the Korean market context (agency structure, media ecosystem, client decision-making patterns).
        * **③ Launch Monthly "Global → Local" Sessions:** Institutionalize a monthly session where success stories from other markets are shared with the Korean team and application plans are discussed. (A bidirectional structure where Korean success stories are also exported globally, not just one-way transfer)
    *   ✅ **Purpose:** "Don't reinvent the wheel." Rapidly transplant proven global success formulas to Korea to minimize trial-and-error costs.

3.  **Establish Weekly Cadence + Lay Right Build Foundation (→ Risk #3 Preemptive Response)**
    *   Instead of complex meeting structures, set the team's execution rhythm with exactly **3 weekly routines**, while naturally deriving **'Right Person' criteria** needed for future team expansion:

    | Day | Meeting | Participants | Duration | Purpose |
    | :---: | :--- | :--- | :---: | :--- |
    | **Mon** | Pipeline Review | All Sales + AM | 30 min | Weekly pipeline status check + competency gap observation |
    | **Wed** | 1:1 Coaching | Individual members (rotating) | 30 min | Deal coaching + growth direction discussion (behavior, not results coaching) |
    | **Fri** | Wins & Learns | Entire team (voluntary) | 20 min | Share 1 success story + 1 failure lesson (build organizational learning culture) |

    *   ✅ **Right Build Connection:** After accurately identifying the organization's strengths and gaps through 60 days of weekly routine operation, hiring criteria and team-building roadmaps are concretized in Phase 3. The key sequence is: "First fully understand the current team, then define what kind of person is needed."

### 📅 Phase 3: Lead & Execute (Day 61–90) — "Declare the Vision and Activate the Execution Engine"

> **Principle:** Show the team "where we're going" with concrete numbers, and begin Right Build for growth.

**3 Action Items:**

1.  **Declare the 2026→2030 Growth Vision to the Entire Team (→ Revenue Growth Initiative)**
    *   Based on 90 days of learning, declare a **mid-to-long term vision with specific numbers** at a company-wide town hall:
    *   *"RTB House Korea will achieve $42.2M in revenue in 2026 (16% YoY growth), and by 2030, break through $88.7M to reclaim the #1 position in Korea's performance AdTech market."*
    *   **3 Key Messages:**
        * **① 2026 H2 Offensive:** Launch 44 new campaigns in the second half, surpassing 235 active campaigns.
        * **② Secure Major Clients:** Onboard at least 3 new Tier 1 accounts (Coupang, Samsung Electronics, APR, Nexon — annual budget $3.5M+).
        * **③ Overtake Criteo by 2030:** Maintain 25–30 new launches per quarter to complete the 495 active campaign structure for industry #1.
    *   ✅ **Why numbers?** Korean organizations are more strongly motivated by **clear numerical targets against competitors** than abstract visions. Setting "beat Criteo" as a concrete rival creates cohesion across the entire team.

2.  **Finalize Right Person Hiring Criteria & Begin First Hire (→ Risk #3 Full Response)**
    *   Based on the organizational capability gaps observed over 60 days in Phase 2, finalize the criteria and profile for **the first strategic hire of 2026**:
        * **Top Priority Position:** 1 Senior AE with experience closing mega-accounts ($70K+/month). This single hire creates the breakthrough for major deals among the 44 new H2 launches.
        * **Right Person Criteria:** Evaluate not just experience, but simultaneously: ① Technical understanding of deep learning/programmatic ② Consultative sales capabilities based on A/B testing ③ Cultural fit with the existing team (Kindness, Curiosity, Excellence, Ownership).
    *   ✅ **Risk Defense:** Not "fill the seat quickly" but "fill the seat correctly." The opportunity cost of one wrong hire delays growth by 6 months.

3.  **Activate Delegation Framework (→ Risk #1 Core Talent Leverage)**
    *   Limit the Country Manager's direct management to **2 areas only (Tier 1 strategic accounts + global HQ relations)**, delegating all other operations to team leads.
    *   Grant existing key talent **clear ownership and decision-making authority** to create the sense that "my role is growing in this organization." Micromanagement is the most powerful attrition trigger for senior-level Korean professionals.

### 📅 Phase 4: Scale & Evolve (Day 91–180) — "Make the 2026 Revenue Growth a Reality"

> **Principle:** Scale on validated structures while completing the execution engine for the 2026 H2 offensive.

**3 Action Items:**

**1. Vertical Squad Reorganization — A Structure That Leverages Existing Core Talent (→ Risk #1 + Revenue Target)**

Reorganize the current 20-person team into **3 vertical squads** aligned with industry priorities derived from the [Market Analysis Post](/en/posts/2026-05-17-korea-digital-ad-market-size-analysis). The key is not a large-scale reorganization, but **role redefinition that maximizes existing core talent's strengths**.

| Squad | Composition | Target Accounts | 2026 Mission | Market Rationale |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **🛒 Commerce Squad** | Senior Sales 1 + AM 2 + TAM 1 | Coupang, Musinsa, Olive Young, SSG | Onboard 2 new Tier 1 major clients | E-Commerce = $3.2B–$3.5B digital ad budget (45–50%). Criteo win-back is the fastest growth path |
| **🎮 Gaming & App Squad** | Sales 1 + AM 1 + TAM 1 | Nexon, Krafton, Netmarble | 1 Tier 1 gaming company A/B test success → regular conversion | Gaming budget $1.1B–$1.4B. Prove efficiency vs Moloco/AppLovin via predictive LTV modeling |
| **🌏 K-Brand & Growth Squad** | Sales 1 + AM 1 | APR, Samyang Foods, Yanolja, Toss | Build pipeline for 2 K-Beauty/K-Food global D2C clients | Hybrid K-Brand budget $1.4B–$1.8B. Global CBEC conversion as explosive growth driver |

**2. Launch the 2026 H2 Revenue Initiative — 44 New Campaign Launches**
*   Convert the vision declared in Phase 3 into execution. Achieve **44 new campaign launches** over the 6-month second half to secure 235 active campaigns and $42.2M in revenue by year-end.
*   **"Prove It" Campaign Blitz:** Simultaneously propose 30-day free A/B tests to all Tier 1 targets. Demonstrate iROAS superiority over Criteo to drive budget migration (Scale It).
*   **Country Manager Direct Involvement:** The Country Manager personally attends C-level meetings for Tier 1 major deals (Coupang, Samsung Electronics, APR, Nexon) to elevate relationships to the strategic partnership level.

**3. Launch Regular Global Winning Playbook Operations (→ Risk #2)**
*   Execute the "Global → Local" sessions designed in Phase 2 **at least 3 times** by Day 180 to institutionalize them.
*   Specifically, immediately apply concrete plays from markets (DACH, UK, etc.) that have successfully won back Criteo accounts — what A/B test designs were used to migrate budgets — to the Korean Commerce Squad.

## 4.2 Sales & Account Management Operations

### 🔄 1. Team Collaboration — A Concise Collaboration Framework

Complex processes don't get executed. Simplify Sales-AM collaboration into **exactly 2 rituals**.

**① Pre-flight Handoff (Pre-Launch Campaign Handover)**
*   When Sales closes a deal, they must prepare a **1-page Campaign Brief** before handing off to AM.
*   Included items: Client expected KPIs, contract terms (budget/duration), competitive landscape, client internal decision-maker map
*   ✅ **Korea Optimization:** Since Korean advertisers frequently execute through agencies, it's essential to specify **"whether the final decision-maker is the advertiser or the agency."** This single line dramatically improves AM communication efficiency.

**② Monthly Win/Loss Review**
*   Once a month, 30 minutes. Sales + AM jointly analyze 1 won deal and 1 lost deal from the previous month.
*   ✅ **Rule:** No blame. Discuss only **"how can we win next time?"** — not "why did we lose?"
*   ✅ **Korea Optimization:** In Korean organizational culture, 'sharing failures' can conflict with the face-saving culture. The Country Manager proactively builds psychological safety by sharing their own failure stories first.

**Motivation System:**
*   **Monetary:** Team-wide bonus upon quarterly team target achievement (separate from individual commissions). Team-level rewards prevent silos.
*   **Non-monetary:** Quarterly Top Performer receives the opportunity to **visit the Warsaw HQ R&D Center + participate in global workshops**. Non-monetary growth opportunities aligned with RTB House's core values — **Kindness, Curiosity, Excellence, Ownership** — serve as a particularly powerful retention factor for Korea's younger workforce.
